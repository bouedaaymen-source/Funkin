What Happened in the Reel?

Posted by @twentyninetycreative (Twenty Ninety | Games 🎮), this 32.6-second episode chronicles the moment The Town's 1,900 follower avatars rally around a newly unlocked, cracked dry Stone Golem lying parched on the grass. Following a top comment from @ashrafabisaid asking "DIG A HOLE??", four tan mannequin citizens grab shovels and begin digging a massive dirt pit to find water.
